How to shop smarter on Amazon

  1. Viral doesn't always mean good. Read the 1–3 star reviews before buying, not just the 5-star ones.
  2. Check the seller. Popular products get copied. Look for "Sold by" the brand or Amazon.
  3. Use Subscribe & Save for essentials. Batteries, trash bags, wipes and detergent are often cheaper on a subscription, and you can cancel any time.
  4. Compare unit prices. Bulk packs usually win, but not always. Look at the price per item.
  5. Watch for deal days. Prime Day, Black Friday and Cyber Monday bring big discounts on viral items.
