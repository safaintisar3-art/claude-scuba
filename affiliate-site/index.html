How to build a comfortable home office

  1. Start with the chair and desk. You touch them all day. Your elbows should sit at about 90° with your feet flat on the floor.
  2. Put your screen at eye level. The top of the monitor should be at or slightly below eye height, about an arm's length away.
  3. Fix your lighting. Light from the side or front, never only from behind the screen.
  4. Upgrade audio before video. People forgive a grainy picture; they don't forgive bad sound.
  5. Tame the cables last. A tray and a few ties make a setup feel finished.
